abstract: |-
  基础模型正在重塑统计学研究、实践与教育。本报告将围绕三个方面的进展展开：自主数据分析、预测与生成建模的进步，以及统计方法研发的加速。

  以 LAMBDA 为代表的基于大语言模型（LLM）的智能体，能够实现端到端数据分析的自动化。表格基础模型（TFM）在小规模表格数据集上的表现优于强大的树模型基线；扩散模型与流模型则能够学习图像、基因组序列和分子结构等数据的复杂高维分布。同时，大语言模型也在帮助研究者提出、实现并检验新的统计学思路。

  这些进展对统计方法与理论提出了新的要求。报告最后将对表格基础模型进行分析，比较其与现有方法的统计风险。
abstractEn: |-
  Foundation models are reshaping statistical research, practice, and education. This talk will discuss advances in three areas: autonomous data analysis, predictive and generative modeling, and accelerated development of statistical methods.

  Large language model (LLM) agents such as LAMBDA can automate end-to-end data analysis. Tabular foundation models (TFMs) outperform strong tree-based baselines on small tabular datasets, while diffusion and flow models can learn complex high-dimensional distributions of data such as images, genomic sequences, and molecular structures. LLMs are also helping researchers propose, implement, and test new statistical ideas.

  These advances place new demands on statistical methodology and theory. The talk will conclude with an analysis of tabular foundation models, comparing their statistical risk with that of existing methods.
