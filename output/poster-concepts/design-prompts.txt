ART DIRECTION A — NOCTURNE / futuristic mathematical sculpture.
Near-black midnight blue base, bright warm-white typography, selective acid-lime accent. Large editorial sans typography with tightly controlled alignment. Build a genuinely striking sculptural centerpiece: one delicate luminous folded toroidal surface or a fluid silver-iridescent mathematical ribbon, sweeping at a diagonal through the middle-right; spectral cyan/silver reflections, visually sophisticated physically rendered material, not generic neon circuitry or a starfield. This form is an abstract decorative artwork, no scientific axis labels or fabricated equations.
Composition: small AI+X SEMINAR masthead at top with generous margin. Huge title across upper third with 'Foundation Models' visually prominent in lime. Sculptural fold occupies center; reserve a quiet area to avoid text collisions. In lower middle, speaker name and credentials on left, the source photo in a tall clean rectangular frame on the right, roughly a quarter of poster width. Sparse lime fine rules make hierarchy. Compact talk focus and two-column time/join information footer. Controlled Swiss-grid structure, high contrast, beautiful dark negative space. The fancy quality comes from material, typography, scale and composition; no pill labels or card UI.

ART DIRECTION B — SWISS / bold international typographic poster.
Warm ivory paper ground, ultramarine blue as the dominant type and graphic color, one vivid vermilion-orange accent. Flat precise modernist print design with subtle real paper grain. Extremely large 'AI+X' masthead occupying a strong upper strip; elegant small 'SEMINAR' and Chinese academic descriptor. Main title in very bold condensed sans typography, asymmetric line breaks, dominant size and generous leading.
One massive cropped ultramarine multiplication-sign / intersection form at the right margin gives a strong repeatable series signature; tiny vermilion accent block used deliberately once. Native source portrait is in a simple rectangular crop at mid-left with blue thin frame, speaker typography at mid-right; the face must remain a natural accurate color photograph. Speaker '黄坚' very large and the credentials smaller. Talk-focus row and event footer grounded by thin blue rules. The main composition should be quite different from a dark technology poster: airy, architectural, flat printmaking aesthetic, surprising but balanced alignment, no gradients, no 3D chrome, no scientific network icons. Avoid making a generic corporate flyer. All text remains legible and not inside busy graphical areas.

Rich oxblood / aubergine background with warm parchment text and a restrained pale-lavender accent. A tactile, elegant high-fashion editorial sensibility translated to a serious research seminar. Use a refined high-contrast serif for the enormous English title, paired with minimalist Chinese sans-serif for the speaker and metadata. 'AI+X' at top left as a crisp small wordmark with SEMINAR; very generous margins and dramatic vertical rhythm.
A beautiful abstract translucent pleated glass / folded silk fan sculpture in pale lilac sweeps through the upper-right margin and behind empty space; graceful moiré-like fine texture suggests statistical manifolds, purely decorative with no invented equations. The talk title is large across upper-middle, text and art must not overlap messily. Place the unchanged natural source portrait as a medium rectangular inset near lower-left with a very thin parchment keyline, and '黄坚' with credentials immediately to its right, elegant prominent hierarchy. Lower area has talk-focus text and a minimal event-details footer. It should feel like a museum lecture invitation with daring typography and premium material, distinctly different from both a neon technology poster and a Swiss blue/orange poster. No gold luxury clichés, no ornamental borders, no UI cards.
