E-ligature
Use case: logo-brand.
Create one polished identity exploration sheet for the academic research seminar "AI+X". This is a NEW LOGO CONCEPT, not a poster. A professional, distinctive and intelligent visual identity for interdisciplinary AI scholarship. All spelling must read exactly "AI+X" and optional small "SEMINAR". The capital I and the plus sign must remain unambiguous.
Landscape 3:2 white artboard, 1536x1024 preferred. A small concept letter top-left. One large master logo in the upper two-thirds. Along the bottom, three small carefully spaced functional proofs of the SAME identity: compact avatar symbol, fully black single-ink version, and white reversed version inside a modest solid dark rectangle. Plenty of white space, deliberate optical alignment, no other captions or explanatory copy.
Render crisp flat vector-style artwork, solid ink fills and exceptionally precise typography. No textures, no 3D, no shadows, no gradients, no mockup objects, no business cards, no glow. Avoid a generic AI sparkle, brain, chip, neural network globe, fake university shield, stock atom, generic infinity loop. No fragile details. The icon and letters should remain strong at small sizes. Do not reuse a four-petal segmented X or a 2x2 stack of AI/+X. Develop the one core design idea below thoroughly rather than adding decoration.

Concept letter E. CONCEPT: SCULPTED AX LIGATURE.
Create an original compact monogram of A and X sharing one diagonal structural stroke. This should be a single solid, precise and immediately memorable constructed silhouette, not simply an A printed on an X. A subtle plus-shaped counter-space where the strokes meet can express the connection, but it must not destroy either letter's silhouette. No four disconnected petals. An asymmetric geometric rhythm with one open triangular counter gives it intelligence. Medium-thick robust strokes, restrained angle cuts. Forest green #113D33 with a small warm pale-chartreuse accent only in the wordmark's +. Pair the emblem at left with a beautifully kerned humanist sans "AI+X", small SEMINAR below. The icon should feel bespoke and iconic, like an architecture/research collective mark. Avatar uses the monogram. The fully black proof must preserve every meaningful negative space.

Concept letter F. CONCEPT: MATHEMATICAL NOTATION / OPEN FRAME.
A highly typographic logo built around the exact readable phrase "AI+X", with two distinctive structural mathematical brackets framing it: a low left L-shaped bracket and a high right reversed-L bracket, forming an intentionally OPEN rectangular frame. The brackets and lettering form one tightly integrated custom composition; they are substantial, not hairlines. Letters are a refined medium-weight monospaced/slab hybrid with clear A crossbar and capital I crossbars. The + is the vivid vermilion focus of an otherwise graphite-black logo. Asymmetrical corner brackets signal an open field of inquiry, not a developer-code template. No angle brackets, slashes, programming syntax or code clichés. Small SEMINAR sits outside the open frame below with deliberate alignment. Avatar is the two offset corner brackets enclosing a single visible plus, exceptionally balanced. One-color proof in black, white reversed on graphite. Make this academic, rigorous and distinctive through exact spacing and unexpectedly elegant typography.
