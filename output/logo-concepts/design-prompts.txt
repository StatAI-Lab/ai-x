A-geometric-wordmark
Use case: logo-brand.
Design one refined brand identity concept board for the academic seminar series named exactly "AI+X", optional small descriptor "SEMINAR". It explores interdisciplinary artificial intelligence research for a serious academic audience. The user wants a redesigned logo, not a poster or illustration. This must feel like a thoughtful custom identity from a professional graphic design studio.
Output a landscape 3:2 concept sheet (1536 x 1024 preferred) with a pristine warm-white canvas, crisp flat vector-style geometry, no paper grain. One very large primary logo in the upper two-thirds, and a carefully aligned quiet row in the lower quarter with THREE small functional proofs: compact avatar symbol, one-color black logo, and white logo on a small dark rectangular field. These are variations of the SAME identity, not additional concepts. Use subtle spacing and no captions besides the small concept letter at top-left. No divider boxes except the necessary dark reverse field.
The brand name must read unambiguously as A I + X, visibly preserving the plus sign; never AIX, AlX, or A1X. Clean optical kerning and deliberate stroke relationships. Primary mark must work at small scale and in one ink. Large clear shapes; no fragile hairlines, no generic atomic orbits, no neural networks, no brains, no circuit icons, no generic AI sparkle, no infinity signs, no globes, no stock university crest, no gradients, no glow, no 3D, no shadows, no realistic merchandise or mockups. No taglines or invented facts. Logo artwork is flat, clean and ready to recreate as vector.

CONCEPT A: GEOMETRIC WORDMARK. Place a tiny "A" at top-left.
A tightly art-directed, custom bold geometric sans wordmark "AI+X". The letter A has an unmistakable crossbar, the capital I has simple short horizontal bars so it cannot be mistaken for lowercase l. The plus and X share the same stroke weight and mathematical construction, but remain visibly distinct glyphs. Black / deep ink letters with only the plus in saturated cobalt blue; optional a very small purposeful cobalt cut at the X crossing if it improves the design, but avoid gratuitous cuts. The X is strong and geometrically precise. Premium Swiss editorial research identity, memorable through typography alone. Primary logo is horizontally composed and dominant. Add small spaced "SEMINAR" below in matching sans, secondary to the wordmark. Compact avatar uses an optically balanced 2x2 arrangement of A I on the first row and + X on the second row, preserving this same letter construction. No separate decorative graphic in the main logo.

CONCEPT C: MODULAR MONOGRAM. Place a tiny "C" at top-left.
A bold typographic square monogram constructed from four equally weighted characters, arranged in a precise compact 2x2 matrix:
A I
+ X
It is immediately legible as AI+X; retain the real A crossbar and distinguish I from a dividing line. Charcoal black letters and a single vermilion-orange plus. All elements share a disciplined medium-bold modular stroke system. The monogram's overall contour forms a square but DO NOT enclose it in a square border.
