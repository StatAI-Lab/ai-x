CONCEPT LETTER K. OPEN PAGES / EXCHANGE OF KNOWLEDGE.
Create a distinctive small geometric emblem evoking two OPEN BOOKS intersecting as an abstract X, but do not draw a literal illustrated book. Four broad fanlike page planes converge on one small open central diamond. The planes have subtly uneven outer lengths for a refined asymmetric architectural silhouette. Flat ink-black and muted sage green. Only two or four broad planes, no line clutter, no ordinary four-petal X with plus-shaped counter. It should feel like an original typographic publishing-house / research-forum symbol with knowledge opening in several directions. Pair at left of an elegant slightly condensed medium-weight sans "AI+X", with SEMINAR smaller below. In the black proof retain the opening between the planes. The logo should express learning and exchange with unusual but extremely simple geometry.

CONCEPT LETTER L. CONTOUR FIELD.
A distinct abstract X symbol constructed from exactly FIVE sturdy parallel gently flowing contour bands, continuous ribbon-like lines bending into a central saddle. Flat cobalt blue lines on white, equally thick and generously spaced, no filled heavy X, no gradients, no literal topographic map, no mesh of dozens of hairlines. The contour lines must define a coherent compact X silhouette with an open center, like a crisp mathematical field compressed into a memorable symbol. It must feel contemporary, intelligent and calm rather than retro or hypnotic. Pair the symbol with an understated ink-blue "AI+X" in a refined modern sans, small SEMINAR. The compact avatar is the same five-band icon; all versions must preserve exact contour construction. Put attention on optical spacing and robust stroke thickness for small scale.

CONCEPT LETTER M. DISCRETE SAMPLING.
An original research identity made of a sparse, disciplined arrangement of solid circles creating a clear X silhouette. Exactly 13-17 large circular points with generous consistent spacing, with a small independent plus-shaped center element in vermilion. Point sizes may vary in two deliberate sizes to produce visual rhythm, not random data visualization. Dark graphite circles, one vermilion plus; no connections, no globe, no neural network, no atom, no confetti. The mark suggests discrete samples and intersections using minimal graphic language. Pair with strongly kerned modern "AI+X" typography, small SEMINAR. The icon is compact and memorable at avatar scale. Black proof translates the plus to black without losing its shape. Avoid a generic sparkle outline; the regular diagonal samples must clearly form an X.

CONCEPT LETTER N. HUMANIST LOWERCASE WORDMARK.
An entirely typographic new identity. The main logo reads exactly "ai+x" in LOWERCASE, deliberately different from uppercase research-institute logos. Custom refined rounded slab-serif / humanist letterforms, soft square terminals, a readable double-storey a, a lowercase i with one geometric square dot, an upright true plus, and a beautifully drawn open lowercase x. Compact proportional rhythm. Not childish, not bubbly, not a startup SaaS logo: serious editorial friendliness, contemporary typography for an open academic community. Deep warm plum letters with a terracotta plus. Small uppercase "SEMINAR" below. No detached emblem in main lockup. Avatar is an especially well-balanced a+ ligature in the same type language, maintaining separate readable shapes. Single-ink black and reversed proofs keep exact same lowercase master. Polished typography and smart spacing carry the entire design.
